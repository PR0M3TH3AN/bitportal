What to know

  • Sign-in is BitLogin. It is alpha software: use an identity you can afford to lose until it has had an outside audit.
  • You sign in at your sign-in address, in a window of its own, and nowhere else. No page of BitPortal and no world will ever ask for your password. If one does, leave. Before you type anything, check the address at the top of that window.
  • BitPortal holds no key and no password, has no account server, and stores nothing about you. It may do only what that window listed when you allowed it.
  • You are asked once for BitPortal, and once at each world you choose to sign in to, in the same window, as the same profile.
  • On a phone, a computer or a headset's own screen, it is the same page with the same ways to sign in.
  • From inside VR, this page opens on your headset's flat screen. When you are done, the button back to your room takes you into VR again.
