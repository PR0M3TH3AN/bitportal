What to know

  • Sign-in is BitLogin, drawn in BitPortal's colours. It is alpha software: use an identity you can afford to lose until it has had an outside audit.
  • Your key stays in this browser. BitPortal has no account server and stores nothing about you.
  • You sign in once, here, and you are signed in wherever you go. A world never asks you to sign in to it.
  • You sign in here, on this page, and nowhere else. A world's menu can bring you here and take you back, and that is all it can do: no world will ever ask for your password itself. If one does, leave. Before you type anything, check that the address above is this site's.
  • On a phone, a computer or a headset's own screen, it is the same page with the same ways to sign in.
  • From inside VR, this page opens on your headset's flat screen. When you are done, the button back to your room takes you into VR again.
