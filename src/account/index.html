Signing in doesn't change your lobby yet. Shelves built from the people you follow come in a later version.

What to know

  • Sign-in is handled by BitLogin, which is alpha software. Use an identity you can afford to lose until it has had an outside audit.
  • Your key stays in this browser. BitPortal has no account server and stores nothing about you.
  • You sign in here, on this page, and nowhere else. No world and no portal will ever ask for your password; if one does, leave.
  • On a headset, set up your account on a phone or computer first. Signing in inside a headset isn't smooth yet.
