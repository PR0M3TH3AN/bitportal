One room, doors to everywhere.

  • Go through a door

    Every door shows where it really leads, and you stop at the Airlock to see the address before you cross.

  • Meet people

    Join the room you are in to see the people there, talk and write to them. Nobody is joined until they choose to.

  • Carry things

    What you hold goes with you from world to world, and only works where a world lets it.

  • Sign in once

    One sign-in at BitLogin, the same in every world. Or look around as a guest.

Make a world of your own.

Copy the world template, change one scene, put it on any web host, and announce it with your own key. It becomes a door in every client that reads the announcement, and nobody has to let you in.
