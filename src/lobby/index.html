A door can't lie about where it goes.

  1. Pick a door

    Each one shows the address it leads to, in full, and whether that site vouches for it.

  2. Stop at the Airlock

    A plain page shows the real address once more and waits for you. Nothing about your trip is sent to a server.

  3. Arrive

    The world opens on its own site. Its way back brings you here, facing the door you left through.

Nobody decides who gets a door.

There is no index to apply to. A world announces itself, and anyone can publish a list of the worlds they like. BitPortal's own list is one of them.

You have a world

Announce it with your own key. From then on it is on the shelf of announced worlds for anyone who asks to see them, and every curator can find it.

Announce a world

For a site you already run, three steps cover it.

You want it on BitPortal's list

Announce it first. Then say so, with its address, and it will be looked at: does the site vouch for the announcement, and does it do what it says.

Ask for a door

That opens a form on GitHub, where the request is public.

You'd rather choose for yourself

Follow anyone's list, and it gets a shelf here. Put BitPortal's away if you like. Which lists you follow stays on this device.
