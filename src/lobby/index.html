A door can't lie about where it goes.

  1. Pick a door

    Each one shows the address it leads to, in full, and whether that site vouches for it.

  2. Stop at the Airlock

    A plain page shows the real address once more and waits for you. Nothing about your trip is sent to a server.

  3. Arrive

    The world opens on its own site. Its way back brings you here, facing the door you left through.

Experimental, and still being built. BitPortal is part of the BitBlocks family and follows the Nostr Spatial Web Protocol.
