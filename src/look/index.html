Your look

How other people see you when you share a room in a world. Pick the parts and the colours. The mark on the chest and back is yours and isn't a choice.

What to know

  • Your look is kept on this device. BitPortal has no server to send it to.
  • A world sees it only when you go there as yourself. The page before each world asks, every time.
  • A look that is the same everywhere makes you recognisable everywhere. Going plain, you are a different figure each visit.
  • A world may dress its visitors its own way. Your mark is the part meant to stay.
