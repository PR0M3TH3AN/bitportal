# The music of BitPortal's room

Four pieces, played in turn, each fading into the next: `room-a.ogg` to
`room-d.ogg`. The owner chose them by ear on 2026-11-18 from pieces made for
BitPortal. Each was made in code, by a synthesizer written for the purpose,
with no model, sample or recording behind it. They are offered under this
repository's licence.

The first to play is `room-b`, the one the owner picked first.

The cues and the room's hum are the NSWP kit's own, in `vendor/nswp/sounds/`,
whose README says where each of those came from.
